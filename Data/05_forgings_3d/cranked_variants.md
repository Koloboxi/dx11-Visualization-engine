# Cranked forgings

In viz, select **Body → Broken line → XZ**. Use Z as the draw direction and Y as the sweep direction. Enter each profile below as an ordered list of (X, Z) points. Coordinates are in millimetres.

The profiles cross rising and descending webs between offset bosses. A plane along the same draw direction cannot cut all three witness sections at the first three bosses. In each model the allowable middle height is separated from the interpolation of the two outer intervals.

The meshes use curvature-adaptive sizing. Mean edge length is 0.57–0.60% of the largest part dimension (0.62% in test-with-holes).
